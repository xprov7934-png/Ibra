Key rules for practical and written work.

📈 Graph plotting

  • Use one smooth best-fit line, not dot-to-dot.
  • Circle anomalous points and exclude them.
  • Gradient triangle should span at least half the line.
  • Label axes as Quantity / unit.
  • Use sensible 1, 2, 5 or 10 scale intervals.

🧪 Food tests

  • Starch → iodine → blue-black.
  • Reducing sugar → Benedict's + heat → brick-red.
  • Protein → NaOH first, then CuSO₄ → lilac/purple.
  • Fat → ethanol then water → cloudy white.

⚗️ Qualitative analysis

  • Cu²⁺ + NaOH → blue precipitate.
  • Fe²⁺ + NaOH → dirty green precipitate.
  • Fe³⁺ + NaOH → reddish-brown precipitate.
  • CO₃²⁻ + acid → gas turns limewater cloudy.
  • Cl⁻ + AgNO₃ → white precipitate.
